COMMENT WARS

A countryball war game played in the comments of our YouTube Shorts.
Narrated by Ballthazar, the chronicler.

How it works

Every Short is one day of the war. Write your orders in the comments. When the day ends, the game is resolved and the next Short shows what happened: battles, betrayals, new nations, fallen empires.

Orders close 23 hours after a Short is published. A new day starts every day at 15:00 UTC.

1. Found a nation

#FOUND Name | Color | Capital

Example: #FOUND Thundaria | navy | Bolt City

  • One nation per YouTube account per season.
  • Names must be fictional: real countries (today's or historical) and offensive names are not accepted.
  • 3–24 characters, Latin letters, numbers, spaces, - and '.
  • Colors: red, crimson, orange, yellow, olive, lime, green, forest, teal, cyan, blue, navy, violet, purple, magenta, pink, brown, gray, black, white. If yours is taken, you get the closest free one.
  • If the map is full, your nation is born as a rebellion inside the largest empire. New nations are protected from attacks for 3 days.

2. Give orders

Only the founder of a nation can give its orders. Your first valid order comment of the day counts.

#ATTACK NationName #DEFEND #ALLY NationName
  • One comment may contain one #ATTACK or #DEFEND, plus one #ALLY.
  • You can only attack a neighbor. #DEFEND gives +25% defense.
  • Alliances need both sides to write #ALLY. Allies help in defense. Attacking an ally is betrayal.
  • No orders for 7 days in a row and your nation dissolves.

Your name on screen

Founders can have their YouTube handle and profile picture shown next to their nation in the videos. Comment #SHOWME (also works inside your #FOUND or order comment). We check it before it appears. Changed your mind? Comment #HIDEME. See the Privacy Policy.

3. Likes are power

The likes on your order comment become power: √likes is added to your nation each day. Battles are decided by power plus a little luck. The loser loses provinces; a nation whose capital falls is gone.

Sudden suspicious like spikes are reviewed by a human. Nobody is disqualified automatically.

Seasons & leagues

A season lasts up to 90 days, or until one nation controls 60% of the continent. Each league is its own world with up to 20 nations; the top 3 move up and the bottom 3 move down after each season.
