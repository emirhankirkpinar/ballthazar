PRIVACY POLICY

Comment Wars · Ballthazar YouTube channel
Effective date: 2 November 2026 (updated 2 November 2026)

Who we are

Comment Wars is a game played in the comments of the Ballthazar YouTube channel. It is run by an individual. Contact: ballthazar@proton.me.

YouTube API Services

Comment Wars uses YouTube API Services to read public comments on our own videos and to upload our videos. By taking part in the game you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Only public information from comments posted on our videos:

  • the comment's ID, text, like count and publish/update times;
  • the commenter's YouTube channel ID and public display name;
  • for nation founders, the public creation date of their YouTube channel (to limit fake accounts).

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, private data, or anything outside our own videos' public comments. This website uses no cookies, analytics or ads.

How we use it

  • To run the game: read commands (#FOUND, #ATTACK…), match orders to the account that founded each nation, and turn likes into power.
  • To detect abuse: unusual like spikes and inappropriate names are reviewed by a human.

Our videos show only nation names chosen by players, never YouTube usernames or channel IDs. We do not sell or share this data with anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Comment text, like counts, display names and channel creation dates are kept for at most 30 days and then deleted.
  • We never keep your channel ID permanently. To enforce "one nation per account per season", we keep only a one-way scrambled code (a salted hash) of the founder's channel ID; it cannot be turned back into your channel ID, and it is deleted 30 days after the season ends.
  • Game results (nation names, provinces, battles, rankings) are kept as the history of the game. They do not contain YouTube data that identifies you.

Your choices

  • You can delete your comment on YouTube at any time; deleted comments no longer count.
  • You can ask us to delete all data we hold about your account by writing to ballthazar@proton.me with your channel link. We delete it within 7 days; your nation, if any, is removed from the game.
  • The channel owner's authorization for this app can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings: security.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
